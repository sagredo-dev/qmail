The qmail stack has been deployed.

1. Wait for mariadb to be ready first — every other service waits on it:
     kubectl get pods -w

2. The first qmail pod run does the one-time setup (control files, TLS
   self-signed, DKIM, tcprules, vpopmail domain). Watch its log:
     kubectl logs deploy/qmail -f

   If VQADMIN_PASS was left empty, the generated password is printed to the
   qmail pod stdout on first run.

3. Public ports are bound straight on the node via hostPort
   (qmail: 25/80/465/587, dovecot: 993/995/4190). Open the matching ports on
   the firewall/security list and point your MX/IMAP DNS at the node IP.

4. qmail and dovecot are pinned to the same node (shared mail volumes on
   local-path storage).

Check the whole stack:
   kubectl get deploy,pvc,svc
